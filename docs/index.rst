COE 379L: Responsible Intelligent Systems with Formal Guarantees
================================================================

The goal of this course is to introduce students to algorithms 
and architectures for designing, deploying and evaluating software systems 
enhanced in a responsible way with artificial intelligence (AI). Our approach
will couple the latest machine learning/deep learning (ML/DL) methods, including transformer models, 
with "symbolic" or "formal" methods that allow us to provide mathematical 
guarantees that our system conforms to certain rules. Such hybrid systems are called 
*neuro-symbolic systems* and are capable of improving the quality and reliability of 
systems built with ML/DL alone. Additionally, these systems find applications to research 
in various theoretical fields, including mathematics, computer science, physics, etc.
