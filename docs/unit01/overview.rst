Unit 1: Course Overview and Introduction to AI Systems
======================================================

In this first unit, we will provide an introduction to modern AI and ML techniques, 
including deep learning architectures such as convolution neural networks (CNNs) and transformers. 
We will use the first week of class to provide an overview of the course and to provide students 
with the computing environment at TACC that they will use for completing class work.
